HOW TO
PLAY?

Dragon City is an idle game where players raise dragons and build a floating island realm. Dragons produce gold to upgrade buildings and habitats. Farms yield food to level up dragons, boosting their power and gold output.

Two dragons at Level 4 or higher can be bred to hatch hybrids with random element combinations. Compete in League Battles to earn gems, food, and exclusive rewards, or participate in special events to collect rare dragons.

At Level 27, players unlock the Ancient World to summon ancient dragons using platinum. The game features 22 elements and 7 rarity categories ranging from Common to Mythical and Heroic.

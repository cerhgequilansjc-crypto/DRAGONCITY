Mobile Legends: Bang Bang, commonly known as ML or MLBB, is a fast-paced mobile multiplayer online battle arena (MOBA) game developed and published by Moonton, a subsidiary of ByteDance. It was first released publicly in Brazil on 22 July 2016.

Since launch, it has become one of the most played mobile games globally, especially across Southeast Asia. It has exceeded 1 billion downloads and reached 100 million monthly active players. By 2021, the game had earned over US$1 billion in gross revenue, with nearly half coming from outside Asia—proving its worldwide appeal.

Matches feature two teams of five players fighting in real time across three lanes. Matchmaking takes seconds, and full matches last roughly 10 minutes. Victory depends entirely on skill, teamwork, and strategy—there is no pay-to-win advantage.

Its success also built a thriving esports scene, with the Mobile Legends Professional League (MPL) running official competitions across Southeast Asia, Latin America, and other regions.
